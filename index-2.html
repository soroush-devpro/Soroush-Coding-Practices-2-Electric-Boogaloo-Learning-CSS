I also have an interesting fact for you all:

In web development, there were 147 predefined color names that browsers recognized (e.g., DarkSalmon, FireRed, DodgerBlue, etc.). In 2014, the 148th color was added, called "RebeccaPurple". This color (#663399) was added to honor the daughter of web pioneer Eric Meyer after her passing (Eric's daughter was named Rebecca, and her favorite color was purple). The color of this text is Rebecca Purple!
